# Privacy Policy - Kingsbridge Immigration Chatbot

Last updated: August 2026

This chatbot ("Cyrus") is operated by Kingsbridge Immigration to assist with inquiries via Facebook Messenger and Instagram.

## Information We Collect
When you message our page, we may collect and store:
- The content of your messages
- Your name, email, or WhatsApp number if you provide it
- Your Facebook or Instagram user ID (as provided by the platform)

## How We Use Your Information
We use this information to:
- Respond to your inquiries about Canadian immigration services
- Recommend appropriate consultation services
- Allow our licensed consultants to follow up with you

## Data Storage and Security
Your conversation data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized Kingsbridge Immigration staff.

## Third-Party Services
This chatbot uses Anthropic's Claude AI to generate responses. Message content may be processed by this service to generate replies.

## Your Rights
You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us directly at Kingsbridge Immigration.

## Contact
For questions about this privacy policy, contact Kingsbridge Immigration through our official channels.
